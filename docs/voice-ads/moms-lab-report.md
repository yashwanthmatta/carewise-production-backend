# Mom's Lab Report

Status: **draft for approval**. Not published. Spanish is a draft for a qualified language reviewer.

- **Audience:** Adult children looking after a parent
- **Style:** Family: warm, natural, trustworthy
- **Voice:** Adult narrator, 30s to 50s, warm and unhurried, like a friend who has been through it. No announcer energy. Spanish: neutral Latin American, same warmth.
- **Visuals:** Actor as adult daughter at a kitchen table at night, phone in hand. Screen recordings of the real app with a synthetic sample report only. No real patient data, no real doctor.
- **Music:** Soft solo piano or acoustic guitar, slow (about 70 BPM), no lyrics. Starts after the first line, lifts gently on the brand line.
- **On screen (not spoken):** Synthetic sample report. Actors portrayed. / Not medical advice. Always talk to your doctor.

## Audio mixing

- Voice recorded or generated at 48 kHz, 24-bit, in a quiet room; no clipping (peaks at or below -1 dBTP).
- Final mix about -14 LUFS integrated for social video (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook).
- Music 15 to 20 dB under the voice while anyone speaks; no music over the disclaimer card.
- No sound effects that sound like a phone alarm, siren or heart monitor.
- Captions burned in or uploaded as the .srt here; they must match the spoken words exactly.
- Check on a phone speaker at low volume and on earbuds before approval.

## Required disclosures

- Label any AI-generated voice as AI-generated where the platform asks (Meta, TikTok and YouTube have AI-content labels).
- Use a voice actor's own voice or a stock synthetic voice with a commercial licence. Never clone a real person's voice.
- No testimonials, patient stories presented as real, doctor endorsements or result claims.
- Health-related ads may need platform review or special-category settings; the founder checks each platform's health ad policy before buying.
